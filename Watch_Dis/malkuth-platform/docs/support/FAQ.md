# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

This FAQ covers the most common questions about Malkuth Platform. If you can't find the answer you're looking for, please contact our support team.

## 🌟 General Platform Questions

### What is Malkuth Platform?

**Q: What exactly is Malkuth Platform and how does it work?**

A: Malkuth Platform is an advanced content orchestration system that combines traditional content management with AI-powered bot engagement. It helps content creators by:

- **Content Management**: Upload, organize, and manage video, music, and written content
- **Bot Engagement**: AI personas provide authentic initial engagement to help content gain momentum
- **Analytics**: Comprehensive performance tracking and optimization insights
- **Campaign Management**: Strategic engagement campaigns across multiple phases

The platform uses sophisticated bot personas with realistic personalities and behaviors to create authentic engagement patterns that help quality content find its audience.

### How is this different from other content platforms?

**Q: What makes Malkuth Platform unique compared to YouTube, TikTok, or other platforms?**

A: Malkuth Platform focuses on content amplification rather than hosting. Key differences:

- **Strategic Engagement**: Planned engagement campaigns rather than hoping for organic discovery
- **Quality Focus**: System favors high-quality content over viral but low-value content
- **Creator Control**: Full control over your content and engagement strategy
- **Multi-format Support**: Supports video, music, and written content equally
- **Analytics Depth**: Detailed insights into engagement patterns and optimization opportunities

### Is bot engagement ethical and legal?

**Q: Is using bot engagement ethical? Will this get my content flagged on other platforms?**

A: Yes, when done responsibly. Our approach focuses on:

**Ethical Framework:**
- **Quality Enhancement**: Bots only engage with genuinely good content
- **Authentic Behavior**: Realistic timing, interests, and interaction patterns
- **Transparency**: Clear disclosure when required by platforms
- **No Deception**: Bots don't impersonate real people or spread misinformation

**Platform Compliance:**
- Designed to work within platform terms of service
- Natural engagement patterns prevent detection
- Emergency stop features for risk management
- Regular updates to maintain compliance

## 📤 Content Upload and Management

### What content types are supported?

**Q: What types of files can I upload to Malkuth Platform?**

A: We support three main content categories:

**Video Content:**
- Formats: MP4, MOV, AVI, WebM, MKV
- Max Size: 2GB per file
- Quality: Up to 4K resolution
- Processing: Automatic transcoding to multiple qualities

**Music Content:**
- Formats: MP3, WAV, FLAC, OGG, M4A
- Max Size: 100MB per file
- Quality: Up to 96kHz/24-bit
- Features: Waveform generation, metadata extraction

**Written Content:**
- Formats: Markdown, HTML, Plain Text
- Max Size: 10MB per file
- Features: Live preview, reading time calculation, SEO optimization

### How long does content processing take?

**Q: Why is my content still processing? How long should it take?**

A: Processing times vary by content type and complexity:

**Video Content:**
- 5-10 minutes: Short videos (under 10 minutes)
- 15-30 minutes: Medium videos (10-60 minutes)
- 30-60 minutes: Long videos (over 1 hour)
- Additional time for 4K content and complex formats

**Music Content:**
- 2-5 minutes: Most audio files
- 5-10 minutes: High-quality/long-form audio
- Additional time for waveform generation

**Written Content:**
- Immediate: Most text content
- 1-2 minutes: Large documents with images

**Factors affecting processing time:**
- File size and complexity
- Server load
- Quality settings
- Number of output formats

### Can I edit content after uploading?

**Q: I need to change my video/content after uploading. Is this possible?**

A: Content editing capabilities vary by type:

**Metadata Editing (Always Available):**
- Title and description
- Tags and categories
- Thumbnail images
- Publication status

**Content Editing:**
- **Written Content**: Full editing with version history
- **Video Content**: Metadata only; replace file for content changes
- **Music Content**: Metadata only; replace file for content changes

**Best Practices:**
- Review content carefully before publishing
- Use draft mode for content under development
- Consider version control for major changes

### Why was my content rejected or flagged?

**Q: My content upload failed or was flagged. What went wrong?**

A: Common reasons for content issues:

**Technical Issues:**
- File size exceeds limits
- Unsupported file format
- Corrupted file upload
- Network interruption during upload

**Content Policy Violations:**
- Copyrighted material without permission
- Inappropriate or harmful content
- Spam or low-quality content
- Misleading titles or descriptions

**Quality Issues:**
- Very low resolution or quality
- Excessive compression artifacts
- Audio sync issues
- Incomplete or corrupted files

**Resolution Steps:**
1. Check file format and size requirements
2. Review content guidelines
3. Try uploading again with a stable connection
4. Contact support if issues persist
